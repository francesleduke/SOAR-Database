Research 101

Curious about what research is, how it works, and what it entails? This page is your starting point for understanding the basics of research.

The Basics

What is Research?

Research is a process of asking and testing questions about the world around us. It encompasses many different types of projects, purposes, and methods — and can be conducted in any field, from the sciences to the arts.

Purpose

Why Does It Matter?

Research helps answer questions that impact people's lives. The answers researchers find shape policies, products, treatments, and public programs. Community voice is central — when people share their needs, research becomes more relevant to the people it aims to serve.

Learn more →
Process

What are the Basic Steps?

  1. Identify a question What problem or gap in knowledge needs to be addressed?
  2. Review existing research What has already been studied and what has been found?
  3. Form a hypothesis What do you expect to find, or what do you want to learn?
  4. Design the study Decide how you'll collect information to test your hypothesis.
  5. Collect data Gather information through experiments, surveys, interviews, or other methods.
  6. Analyze and share Interpret the data and communicate your findings with others.
