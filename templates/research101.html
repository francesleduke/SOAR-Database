What is Research?

Research is the process of asking questions and exploring possible answers. It’s not just labs or data—it’s curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking.

Research can take many forms: running experiments, analyzing texts, building models, creating art, or investigating social systems. At its core, research is about discovering something new—or seeing something familiar in a new way.
