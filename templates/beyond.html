Beyond COA

Research opportunities extend far beyond campus. Learn how to explore, apply, and connect with programs across the country and around the world.

What does “Beyond COA” mean?

While COA has strong partnerships with local organizations, many students also pursue research opportunities elsewhere. These can include summer programs, internships, fellowships, and independent research with external institutions.

Exploring beyond COA allows you to expand your network, experience new environments, and deepen your work in your field of interest.

How to Find Opportunities

🔍

Search Databases

Use curated platforms and research databases to find open opportunities.

📧

Reach Out

Email labs, professors, or organizations doing work that interests you.

🌐

Use Your Network

The CEL can connect you with faculty, peers, and alumni about opportunities they know.

🧭

Follow Your Interests

Look for work aligned with your passions, even if it's outside your subject focus.
