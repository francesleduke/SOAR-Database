Partner Organizations

Students at COA have had long-standing opportunities and relationships with several organizations and institutions. These include:

Jackson Laboratory

A leading biomedical research institution offering students hands-on experience in genetics, genomics, and data science.

MDI Hospital

A community-based hospital providing opportunities in healthcare, patient services, and clinical support roles.

Bigelow Laboratory for Ocean Sciences

A world-renowned oceanographic research institute focused on microbial oceanography and global climate systems.

Mount Desert Rock

A remote research station offering immersive fieldwork experiences in marine ecology and seabird conservation.

Great Duck Island

A unique field research site where students study seabirds, ecosystems, and long-term environmental changes.
