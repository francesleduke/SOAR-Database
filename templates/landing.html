RAVEN

Research Access, Visibility, and Exploration Network

RAVEN is a platform designed to connect undergraduate students at College of the Atlantic with information, resources, and accessible opportunities in research.

RAVEN aims to be a centralized location for students to explore and learn about research at COA, allowing for access and visibiity to span across all fields of interest, experience levels, and backgrounds.
