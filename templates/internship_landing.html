What does it mean to do research in Human Ecology?

Doing research in human ecology means studying the complex, interconnected relationships between human beings and their environments. It investigates how our social, natural, and built surroundings impact our well-being, and how our daily behaviors—like resource use and design choices—affect those environments in return
